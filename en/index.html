2. Core principle: your data stays on your device

Monigo is designed to minimise data. Your financial data and the bank statements you upload are processed and stored exclusively on your device. There is no user account, no sign-in and no central server to which your transactions or statements are automatically transmitted.

Personal data leaves your device only in three clearly defined cases described below: when you voluntarily submit anonymised reconciliation data (Section 4) or a format sample of a bank statement (Section 5), each of which you trigger yourself, and when processing a subscription (Section 6).

3. What data is processed

3.1 Financial data you enter

Transactions, amounts, categories, notes and similar information you enter in the app are stored locally on the device (Apple SwiftData). They are not transmitted to us or to third parties.

3.2 Bank statement import

When you import a bank statement (PDF), it is read on the device and reconciled with the transactions you have entered. Neither the statement nor the data derived from it leaves the device in this process. If Monigo cannot read a statement, or cannot read it reliably, the app prepares a cleaned format sample that you can voluntarily send to us (Section 5); unless you send it, it does not leave your device.

3.3 On-device AI enrichment

On supported devices, Monigo may use an on-device AI (Apple Foundation Models) to improve category and name suggestions for reconciliation. This processing takes place exclusively on the device; no cloud call is made. If the feature is not available on your device, the app continues to work unchanged using local values.

4. Voluntary submission of anonymised reconciliation data

To improve the quality of automatic reconciliation, Monigo lets you send the data from your last reconciliation run to us in anonymised form.

  • Voluntary and explicit: Data is only sent if you actively tap "Send anonymous reconciliation data" in the settings. Nothing is transmitted without that tap. In beta versions (TestFlight), the app additionally offers to send the data after a reconciliation is completed; even then, nothing is transmitted until you explicitly confirm.
  • Preview: Before sending, you can use "Preview the data" to see exactly which data would be transmitted.
  • What is transmitted: amounts, dates, booking text that has been cleaned (redacted) of personal patterns, the respective match result (e.g. "matched automatically" / "open") and the app version. The booking text comes from your bank statement and therefore typically contains merchant or payee names.
  • What is removed: Before sending, recognisable personal information is removed — in particular your private transaction names and notes, IBANs, email addresses and long digit sequences (e.g. card or reference numbers).
  • Transmission method: Sending is done via your email app to monigo_app@icloud.com or via the share option you choose. When sending by email we inevitably see your sender address; we do not use it to link the data to you, and we delete the email or sender information after analysis.
  • Purpose: Solely to analyse and improve the reconciliation algorithm.
  • Legal basis: Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw this consent at any time with effect for the future by not sending further data; data already sent is not affected.

Note: The cleaning is pattern-based and carried out with the utmost care, but in individual cases it cannot be guaranteed that a booking text contains no remaining personal data. In particular, names in the statement's booking text (e.g. for transfers to private individuals) are not removed automatically. The preview lets you check this before sending.

5. Voluntary submission of a format sample

If Monigo cannot read a bank statement (unknown format) or cannot reconcile the total of the transactions it read with the statement's account balance, the app offers to send us a format sample of this statement. We use it to improve reading statements from your bank.

  • Voluntary and explicit: Data is only transmitted if you actively tap "Send anonymous sample" or "Share anonymous sample" and confirm sending.
  • In beta versions (TestFlight): The format sample is prepared with every import and offered together with the anonymised reconciliation data (Section 4) via "Send anonymous data" – even if the balance check succeeds. Here too, nothing is transmitted until you explicitly tap.
  • Preview: "Preview the data" shows you beforehand exactly what would be sent.
  • What is transmitted: the text read from the statement PDF in cleaned form – including dates, amounts, balances and booking texts (which typically contain merchant or payee names) – plus the app version, the page count, the reason for the offer and the reading methods involved. The PDF itself is never transmitted.
  • What is removed: the name and address in the letterhead; names recognised there as the account holder, wherever they appear in the statement; as well as IBANs, email addresses and long digit sequences (e.g. account, card or reference numbers).
  • Transmission method: as described in Section 4, by email to monigo_app@icloud.com or via the share option you choose.
  • Purpose: Solely to improve reading bank statements and reconciliation.
  • Legal basis: Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future by not sending further samples.

Note: This cleaning is also pattern-based. With unusually structured statements, a name or address may remain in individual cases; names of payees or payers in booking texts are not removed automatically. Please check the preview before sending.

6. Subscriptions (Monigo Pro)

Paid features are unlocked via a subscription. Payment is processed through your App Store account with Apple; we receive no payment data such as credit card numbers.

To manage entitlements we use the service RevenueCat. This processes an anonymous, randomly generated app user identifier and the purchase status in order to unlock purchased features and to restore purchases.

  • Apple (App Store): https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

The legal basis is the performance of the contract to provide the app's features (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

7. TestFlight (beta phase)

While Monigo is distributed via Apple's TestFlight, Apple processes data such as your email address and usage and crash information as part of your beta participation. This processing is carried out by Apple; Apple's privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

8. No tracking, no advertising

Monigo contains no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking tools and no cross-device user profiling.

9. Retention period

Your locally stored data is retained for as long as the app is installed on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes the locally stored data. Voluntarily submitted anonymised reconciliation data and format samples are kept only for as long as necessary to improve reconciliation and statement reading.
