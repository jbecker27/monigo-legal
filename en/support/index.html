Help & Support – Monigo

Got a question, found a bug or have an idea? Write to monigoapp@versteckmich.de. I usually reply within a few days.

For bugs, it helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version and Monigo version (at the bottom of Settings in Monigo) and a short description of what you did. Please don't email real bank statements.

Statements & reconciling

Which banks are supported?

Monigo reads PDF bank statements right on your iPhone. ING and Sparkasse are tested with real statements, and Monigo recognises many other layouts with a general reader. The balance check shows whether everything was read correctly: it verifies that the statement's transactions add up from the opening to the closing balance. You can also import the CSV export from your online banking. For an unknown CSV format, Monigo asks once which column contains what.

My statement isn't recognised. What can I do?

Scanned or photographed statements contain no readable text. It's best to download the statement as a PDF directly from your online banking, or to use the CSV export. If Monigo can't read a format reliably, the app offers to send me a cleaned-up format sample so I can add support for it. This is optional. What it contains is explained in the Privacy Policy (section 5).

Reconciling doesn't find a transaction.

Monigo matches transactions by amount and date, even when the bank posts them a few days after your purchase. If the amount differs (e.g. a tip) or the dates are far apart, the transaction stays open. You can then add it as a new entry with Add or ignore it.

My balance in Monigo doesn't match my bank.

Go to Settings → Adjust balance and enter the balance shown by your bank. Monigo records the difference as an adjustment and leaves your existing entries unchanged.

Logging

How do I set up the Shortcut?

Go to Settings → Set up shortcut for step-by-step instructions. After that, you can log expenses with a Shortcut or Siri without opening the app.

Data & backups

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There's no account and no server holding your transactions, and Monigo doesn't sync via iCloud. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I back up my data or move to a new iPhone?

Under Settings → Data Backup → Export Backup you save all transactions, categories, recurring transactions and reconciliations as a file, for example in the Files app. On your new iPhone, choose Import Backup and decide whether to merge or replace the data. Important: if you delete Monigo, the data on the device is gone. Back it up first.

How do I delete my data?

Delete Monigo from your iPhone. Since there's no account, I don't hold any transactions that would need deleting. You can turn off the anonymous usage statistics under Settings → Privacy → Anonymous usage statistics.

Monigo Pro & subscription

What's free, and what does Monigo Pro cost?

Logging, insights and Shortcuts are free. When reconciling, Monigo matches the first 10 transactions of every statement for free. Monigo Pro unlocks full statements. Monigo Pro is available monthly, for 3 months or yearly. You'll see the prices in your currency in the app.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. You can cancel in Monigo under Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription, or in the iPhone Settings app under [your name] → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

My purchase isn't recognised.

Under Settings → Subscription, tap Restore purchases. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by me. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
